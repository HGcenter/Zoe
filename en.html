01 / ABOUT

Working with people.
Understanding organizations.

My path spans global talent development and culture integration at Lenovo, business partnering at DiDi and Alibaba Cloud, and HR leadership at HAOMO.AI. These settings grounded my work in organization design, talent, performance and manager development.

Since 2023, my practice at HG Think Center has supported founders, managers and professionals through greater responsibility, delegation, collaboration and career transitions. I also provide coaching and mentoring in English and Mandarin through global coaching platforms including BetterUp and Pack, with over 1,000 hours accumulated in total.

I connect organizational practice, coaching conversations and founder research through practical handbooks and working tools. My two original written works, Heguang Lianhuan and Heguang Founder, have completed copyright registration in China.

JUDGMENT

ONGOING RESEARCH

How does a founder’s judgment become organizational capability?

Study founder biographies and company cases, comparing critical choices, leadership, delegation and organizational mechanisms. Explore how AI can support research synthesis, structured reflection and decision records, then bring those insights back into coaching, advisory work and tool design.

Founders & decisionsCoaching-informed managementAI-assisted judgment

Tools & learning prototypes

Translate professional knowledge into workflows and interactive designs, with clear descriptions of their current implementation stage.

01
Knowledge framework & local prototype

Founder OS / Organization Diagnosis Copilot

Turn ambiguous organizational problems into clarifying questions, issue classification, framework matching and a 30-day system experiment.

Explore the concept+

Examine the founder, team and organizational systems together, then structure a diagnosis, interview questions and action plan. Current work focuses on knowledge models and local prototypes; it is not a production AI service.

02
Static front-end MVP

IC → Manager Transition Workbench

Connect an initial diagnosis, seven days of action, management scenarios, conversation guides and reflection for new managers.

Explore the product design+

The prototype supports profiles, daily actions and local progress tracking. Its Coach module currently uses a local rules engine, without a live LLM API; the focus is on translating management learning into daily practice.

03
Static information tool

Global Labor Law Directory

Organize country and jurisdiction navigation around official labor-law and government sources for international HR research.

Explore the design principles+

Organize information by topic, jurisdiction and official source, with traceability and clear information layers. The tool provides research entry points; employment decisions still require current rules and local professional advice.

04
Independent Chinese-language research prototype

Founders Chinese Reader

Organize founder stories from Founders Podcast into structured Chinese readings, profile cards and themes, connecting episode research, search and personal notes.

Explore the research prototype+

Uses public RSS to index episodes, with original audio links, founder profiles, reading paths, thematic overviews and local notes. Chinese interpretations are independent research, exploring how biographies and venture cases can become a growing research resource.

05
Local research & content production tool

Key Decision Workbench · 当时

Bring interview topics, source evidence, research briefs and production into one workspace, connecting source verification with personal interpretation.

Explore the workbench+

The local workbench reads an existing research library and supports search and filtering, original video and transcript evidence, research briefs, saved drafts, video import, editing, subtitle burn-in, and video and cover export. Publishing copy can be saved and copied; the user completes publication on the platform.
